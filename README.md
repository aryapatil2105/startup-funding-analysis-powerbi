# 🚀 Indian Startup Funding & Ecosystem Dashboard (2020–2025)

An end-to-end interactive **Power BI** dashboard offering an executive-level summary and granular analysis of the Indian startup funding landscape from **2020 to 2025**.

This project analyzes capital distribution across funding stages, top-performing cities, dominant industry sectors, top venture capital investors, and leading funded startups.

---

## 📊 Executive Overview & Key Metrics

* **Total Funding:** **$28.08B** (₹28.0844bn) across 2020–2025
* **Total Startups Analyzed:** **176**
* **Total Investors Tracked:** **401**
* **Average Funding per Startup:** **$27.06M** (₹27.06M)

---

## 🖼️ Dashboard Preview & Key Insights

The Power BI report contains three distinct analysis views:

### 1. Startup Funding Analysis (Macro Trends)
- **Funding Trend Over Time:** Historical funding trajectory peaking at **$6.0B in 2021 and 2024**, with a total of **$2.8B allocated in 2025**.
- **Stage Distribution:** Capital allocation heavily concentrated in **Late Stage ($23B)** compared to Growth ($4B), Alternative Funding, and Early Stage investments.
- **Top Geographic Hubs:** **Pune ($4.4B)** leads total funding, followed by **Kolkata ($3.6B)**, **Delhi ($3.4B)**, **Gurugram ($3.1B)**, and **Chennai ($2.7B)**.

### 2. Investor Landscape & Market Insights
- **Top Venture Capital Investors:** Led by **Accel ($1,358M)**, **Zodius Capital ($1,010M)**, **Mirae Asset ($967M)**, **Blume Ventures ($889M)**, and **Y Combinator ($856M)**.
- **Stage Distribution by Count:** Early Stage deals account for **47.4%** of total deal count, followed by Growth Stage at **32.47%** and Late Stage at **14.35%**.
- **Top Funded Startups:** Highlights top capital raisers including **NextSense ($1,003M)**, **HomeFoods ($916M)**, **Unacademy ($714M)**, **CoreSpace ($709M)**, and **NeoSense ($641M)**.

### 3. Executive Dashboard
- Combines high-level key performance indicators (KPIs) with industry treemaps (FoodTech, Retail, Consumer Electronics, Mobility, Media) and VC investor benchmarks for quick stakeholder decision-making.
